Safety

App: Thai Jashe! (થઈ જશે!) — Got a job? Thai Jashe! • ગુજરાતી • हिन्दी

For customers

  • Every pro’s photo, identity and police verification are checked. Look for the “Police verified” badge.
  • Check that the person matches the photo in the app before giving the OTP.
  • Use “Share” in an ongoing booking to send the pro’s details to a family member.
  • If you feel unsafe, tap 🆘 SOS — it calls 112 and sends your location to the admin immediately.
  • Never pay more than the app bill; never tell the OTP on the phone.

For pros

  • The customer’s rating is shown in the booking. If a customer misbehaves, rate them and complain.
  • If you feel unsafe, tap 🆘 SOS on the job screen.

Emergency numbers

Police/ambulance/fire: 112 • Women (Abhayam): 181 • Cyber fraud: 1930
