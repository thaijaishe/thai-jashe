Last updated: 13 ઑક્ટોબર 2026. By registering as a pro on “Thai Jashe!” you accept these terms.

1. Independent partner

You are an independent service provider (gig worker), not an employee. You decide when to go online and which job to accept.

2. Registration and verification (KYC)

  • Correct name, Google account (or SMS OTP) login, mobile, photo, Aadhaar number and a photo of the Aadhaar card are mandatory.
  • The Aadhaar number is used only to check it is valid; the app keeps only “XXXX XXXX last 4” and a secret hash — the full number is not stored (UIDAI guidance).
  • Photos of the Aadhaar card and police verification certificate are kept on a secure server (Google Firebase) and are visible only to admins, never to customers.
  • A police verification certificate is required; until then the profile may stay “pending approval”. The admin approves after seeing the originals.

3. Commission and accounts

  • No commission right now. The full amount the customer pays is yours. If commission is introduced later, you will be told in the app at least 30 days in advance and it applies only after you accept the new terms.
  • Income tax/GST on your income, where applicable, is your responsibility.

4. Location, IP and device information

While you are online, your GPS location is taken periodically to give you nearby jobs, show live tracking to the customer and prevent fraud. IP address and device details are recorded at login. When you go offline, location collection stops. Details are in the privacy policy.

5. Conduct

  • Reach on time, never start without the OTP, always make the bill in the app.
  • Never charge more than the app bill. Use the customer’s number only for the job.
  • Intoxication, misbehaviour, theft or calling customers outside the app will lead to a permanent block and, if needed, a police report.

6. Rating

If your average rating falls below 3.5 or you cancel often, your account may be suspended for some time after a warning.

7. Closing the account

You may delete your account anytime from Profile.

8. Social security

As per the Code on Social Security 2020, the company will help you register as a gig worker on e-Shram/government portals.

9. Disputes

Raise customer disputes in the app. Jurisdiction: Vadodara, Gujarat.
