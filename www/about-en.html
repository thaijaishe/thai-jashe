About us

App: Thai Jashe! (થઈ જશે!) — Got a job? Thai Jashe! • ગુજરાતી • हिन्दी

Thai Jashe! (થઈ જશે!) is Gujarat’s own home-services app. For 29 kinds of work — AC, plumber, electrician, carpenter, painting, cleaning and more — a nearby, verified pro reaches your door within minutes.

How we started

The app was started by an AC repair technician. Pros need steady, fair work and customers need someone they can trust. “Thai Jashe!” connects the two. Our motto: Got a job? Thai Jashe! (It will get done!)

What makes us different

  • Fair to pros: customers pay the pro directly (cash). Right now the app takes no commission at all — the full amount goes to the pro.
  • Verified pros: approval only after identity, photo and police verification.
  • Safe start with OTP: work starts only after the customer gives the OTP.
  • Gujarati, Hindi and English: use the app in the language you like.
