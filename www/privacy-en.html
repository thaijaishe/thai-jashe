Privacy policy

App: Thai Jashe! (થઈ જશે!) — Got a job? Thai Jashe! • ગુજરાતી • हिन्दी

Last updated: 13 ઑક્ટોબર 2026. Data Fiduciary: થઈ જશે! (Thai Jashe!) સર્વિસીસ, ગુજરાત. This policy follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the IT Rules 2011/2021.

1. What we collect

  • Customers: name, Google account (or SMS OTP) login, mobile, area, addresses, bookings, problem description, ratings, in-app messages. GPS location while booking (if you allow) — to find pros within 25 km and pin your address.
  • Pros: name, mobile, photo, service, experience, area, Aadhaar (last 4 digits + secret hash only), photos of the Aadhaar card and police certificate (admins only), police verification number, earnings records.
  • Notifications (push): your phone’s notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for new requests/bookings. For a new request the pro’s phone rings for 1 minute.
  • Pro location: GPS location while online (about every minute, more often while travelling to a customer) and its history.
  • Technical data (everyone): login/app-open time, IP address (recorded on our server), device/browser type, device ID (a random number created by the app), network type.
  • Complaints and SOS: ticket details, location at the time of SOS.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS or gallery.

2. Why

  • Finding a nearby pro, completing the booking, showing live tracking.
  • Safety of customers and pros, SOS, preventing fraud and multiple fake accounts (IP/device).
  • bills, resolving complaints, legal duties (government/police requests).

3. Who can see it

  • Only after a booking is accepted do the customer and pro see each other’s number and address. While on the way, the pro’s location is visible only to that customer.
  • IP, device, login and location history and Aadhaar/police documents are visible only to authorised admins. Everyone can see only their own data (enforced by server security rules).
  • Data is stored on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging); Cloudflare servers send notifications and record IPs. Maps are from OpenStreetMap. We do not sell data. It is shared with government agencies only on a lawful written request.

4. How long

  • Profile: while the account is active.
  • Login/IP logs: at least 1 year (DPDP Rules 2025) and 180 days (CERT-In).
  • Location and login history: 1 year, then deleted automatically. Aadhaar/police photos: while the account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Bookings and bills: as required by law (6–8 years for GST), without your name.

5. Your rights

  • Access, correct or delete your data; withdraw consent (you can turn off location in phone settings — a pro may then get fewer jobs).
  • Delete account: in the app, Profile → “Delete account”, or email thaijaishe@gmail.com.
  • Complaints: Grievance Officer ફરિયાદ અધિકારી, થઈ જશે!, thaijaishe@gmail.com. If not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, limited admin access and logs. In case of a data breach we will inform the Board and affected persons within 72 hours.

7. Children

Accounts are not for persons under 18.
