Cancellation & refund policy

App: Thai Jashe! (થઈ જશે!) — Got a job? Thai Jashe! • ગુજરાતી • हिन्दी

Last updated: 13 ઑક્ટોબર 2026. On “Thai Jashe!” payment is made directly to the pro after the job, so no money is taken in advance in the app.

Customer cancels

  • Before a pro accepts, or while on the way: free.
  • After the pro reaches your home: only the visit charge is payable to the pro.
  • Accounts that repeatedly cancel at the last minute may be restricted.

Pro cancels / does not come

  • The booking is automatically sent to other nearby pros; no charge to the customer.
  • If a pro is late or does not come, complain — action will be taken.

Overcharged / work not done properly

  • If more than the app bill was taken, or the same fault returns within 7 days, create a ticket from “Help / complaint”.
  • After investigation: free re-work by the pro, or we try to get the extra amount refunded by the pro.
  • Money is paid directly to the pro and never held by the app — so refunds come from the pro. Any amount refunded through the app is credited to bank/UPI within 7 working days.
